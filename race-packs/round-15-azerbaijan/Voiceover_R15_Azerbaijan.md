# SAVI F1 League | Round 15 Azerbaijan voice-over

Round 15 took us to Baku, and Azerbaijan delivered another street-race mess. George Russell controlled much of the race from pole, but multiple incidents kept pulling the field back together. Max Verstappen closed right onto him in the final laps, but Russell held on by just two tenths of a second for his third win of the season. Verstappen finished second, with Isack Hadjar third, while six drivers failed to finish, including Lando Norris.

Now to the SAVVY League, where Louise Mihalev is on a serious charge. We Are the Competition wins Azerbaijan with 216 points, making it back-to-back victories after Madrid. Louise has now won four of the last six rounds, with five podiums across that stretch. She finished sixth in last year's championship, but Baku moves her into third this season, ahead of Fooch and only 45 points behind JR10044.

Peter Murenu delivers one of the surprises of the round, taking second for Howdy Audi with 208 points. It is Peter's first podium of the season. Then comes defending champion Phil Motteram in third on 202. Phil was eleventh two races ago, but second in Madrid and third in Baku gives him back-to-back podiums and launches Blue Flag Racing four places up the championship to seventh.

At the top, Max Cutter only finishes seventeenth in the Baku round, but JR10044 has an even tougher day in twenty-eighth. Max still gains another 22 points and stretches the championship lead from 54 to 76. Four races ago, Max arrived at the Netherlands 24 points behind JR. He is now 76 ahead. That is a 100-point championship swing in four rounds.

And Fooch's strange relationship with fourth place continues in a new way. After four fourth-place race finishes this season, Baku finally breaks the run, but not positively. Box Box Box finishes twenty-ninth with 137 points and drops from third to fourth in the championship. Still, that is four places better than Fooch's eighth-place finish last season.

The biggest mover is Christiaan Pietropoli. Petch Racing finishes fifth in Baku and jumps five championship positions, from thirty-third to twenty-eighth. Martin West also continues his quiet climb, following twelfth in Italy and tenth in Madrid with seventh in Azerbaijan, his best race result of the season.

At the other end, Richard Saing drops three places to tenth after finishing twenty-first in the round. Last year's third-place championship finisher is suddenly back in the midfield fight. Sam Linke and Alan Thomas both fall four positions, although Alan's FantasyGP total also includes a 40-point cumulative correction, so that drop is not purely race performance.

Fifteen rounds complete. Max has opened some breathing room, Louise has taken over third, the defending champion is charging back, and the midfield has shifted again.

Next stop, Bahrain.
