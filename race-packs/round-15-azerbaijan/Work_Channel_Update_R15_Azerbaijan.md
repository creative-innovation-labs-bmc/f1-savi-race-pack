The fight around seventh has changed dramatically. Phil Motteram jumps four places to seventh and is only **two points ahead of Adrian Jenkins** in eighth. Simon Rogerson is 25 points behind Phil, with Richard Saing another 16 points back in tenth.

Other notable rises: Phil Motteram **+4** to seventh; Jon Evans **+2** to 13th; Kevin de Silva **+2** to 35th.

**Biggest falls:** Sam Linke **-4** to 32nd and Alan Thomas **-4** to 39th. Richard Saing drops **-3** to tenth.
