# Round 14 Madrid voice-over

Round 14 took us to Madrid, and Formula 1’s first race at the new Madring delivered a strategy swing that changed everything.

Lando Norris started from pole and looked in control, building a gap at the front. But when Lance Stroll stopped and triggered a Virtual Safety Car, Mercedes and Red Bull got the cheap stop while Norris missed the window. A slow seven-second pit stop made things worse, dropping him behind Kimi Antonelli and Max Verstappen.

Antonelli held on to take his eighth win of the season, Verstappen finished second and Norris recovered to third.

Now to the SAVI League, where Louise Mihalev has done it again.

We Are the Competition wins Madrid with 185 points, giving Louise her third victory of the season and her fifth podium overall.

Phil Motteram delivers a huge result for Blue Flag Racing, finishing second on 181 and collecting his second silver of the season.

And championship leader Max Cutter takes third with 176. It is Max’s first bronze, his fifth podium of the year, and another strong weekend when the title pressure is starting to build.

Fooch narrowly misses the podium in fourth on 166, while JR10044 finishes fifth on 159.

The midfield was where Madrid really moved things around.

Six competitors shared the biggest rise of the round at two places each: Phil Motteram, Ruan01, Goodie, Martin West, Richard Saing and Nellie Whiteside.

Richard’s result lifts him to seventh overall, but the fight behind him is incredibly tight. Only 10 points now cover seventh through eleventh, and just four points separate Simon Rogerson in eighth from Phil Motteram in eleventh.

At the other end, Matt Magraith had the toughest weekend, dropping three championship places to tenth. Garett Ocampo also slipped two.

And at the front, Max Cutter has strengthened his grip on the championship.

Max leads JR10044 by 54 points, up from 37 after Italy.

Fooch remains third on 2,549, but Louise’s Madrid victory moves her into fourth and closes the gap to just 24 points. Peter Raimondo drops to fifth, only 22 points behind Louise.

That means just 46 points separate third from fifth.

Fourteen rounds complete. Max has breathing room at the top, Louise is charging towards the championship podium, and the midfield is packed tighter than ever.

Next stop, Baku.
