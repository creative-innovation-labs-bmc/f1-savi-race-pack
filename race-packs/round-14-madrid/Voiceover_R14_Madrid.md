# Round 14 Madrid voice-over

Round 14 took us to Madrid, where a Virtual Safety Car and slow stop for pole-sitter Lando Norris opened the door for Kimi Antonelli. Antonelli took his eighth win of the season, with Max Verstappen second and Norris third.

Now to the SAVI League, where Louise Mihalev has done it again.

We Are the Competition wins Madrid with 185 points, giving Louise her third victory and fifth podium of the season. That puts her level with championship leader Max Cutter for both wins and total podiums.

Phil Motteram finishes second on 181, and this one comes with a bit of history.

Phil is the defending SAVI League champion, but this season he has spent much of the year buried in the midfield. Now the champion is starting to look like the champion again. Fifth in Italy, second in Madrid, back-to-back top-five finishes, and four championship positions gained in two rounds. Phil has gone from fifteenth to eleventh and is suddenly right back in the middle of the fight.

Max Cutter takes third on 176, extending his championship lead again.

Then comes Fooch. Fourth. Again.

That is now fourth place in Great Britain, Belgium, the Netherlands and Madrid. Add the shared second in Italy, and Box Box Box has finished inside the top four in five of the last six rounds. Only one podium all season, but enough consistency to stay third overall.

JR10044 finishes fifth and has now gone five consecutive races without a podium since winning Great Britain.

Three races ago, JR arrived in the Netherlands 24 points ahead of Max. Since then, Max has outscored him by 78 points. The championship has swung from 24 behind to 54 ahead.

Behind them, the midfield is packed.

Richard Saing climbs two places to seventh, and he is another familiar name from the sharp end. Richard was a championship podium finisher last season, and after a slower start to 2026 he is beginning to work his way back towards the front. He already has four podiums this year, including second in the Netherlands, and Madrid moves him up another two championship positions.

Simon Rogerson, winner last time out in Italy, holds eighth. Adrian Jenkins moves to ninth, Matt Magraith drops three places to tenth, and Phil is now eleventh.

Only 10 points separate Richard in seventh from Phil in eleventh. Even tighter, just four points cover eighth through eleventh. One strong weekend could completely reshuffle that group.

Martin West is quietly charging too, gaining three championship places in Italy and another two in Madrid. Five positions in two rounds.

Louise is making the same kind of move at the front. Sixth after the Netherlands, fifth after Italy, and now fourth. She has cut Fooch’s advantage for third from 43 points to 24, while Peter Raimondo drops to fifth, only 22 points behind Louise.

Fourteen rounds complete. Max has breathing room, Louise is charging, Fooch keeps finding fourth place, the defending champion is waking up, and last year’s front-runners are starting to reappear.

Next stop, Baku.
