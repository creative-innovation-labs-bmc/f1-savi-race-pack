# 🏁 SAVI F1 League | Round 16 Bahrain

Kimi Antonelli finished second and Lewis Hamilton completed the podium, while George Russell retired late.

Three races ago, after Italy, Louise was 183 points behind Max. Three consecutive wins have reduced that gap to **52 points**, a 131-point swing across Madrid, Azerbaijan and Bahrain.

Fooch remains fourth after finishing 21st in Bahrain. Box Box Box is 143 points behind the tied pair in second, but still four positions higher than Fooch's eighth-place finish last season. Defending champion Phil Motteram stays seventh after a third consecutive top-five race result.
