# SAVVY F1 League | Round 18 USA voice-over

Round 18 took Formula 1 to Austin, where Kimi Antonelli turned pole position into a dominant United States Grand Prix victory. Antonelli controlled the race on a one-stop strategy and finished more than 13 seconds clear of Charles Leclerc. Max Verstappen completed the podium, Lewis Hamilton finished fourth and Lando Norris took fifth, while a late Virtual Safety Car caused by Valtteri Bottas briefly interrupted the closing laps. It was Antonelli's ninth win of the season and moved him to the brink of the world championship.

Now to the SAVVY League, where the title lead has changed hands. Matt Magraith and Louise Mihalev share the USA victory on 189 points. Competition ranking means there is no second place, with Andries van der Merwe third on 178.

For Matt, this completes the full podium set. He already had a second in Canada and a third in Miami; Austin gives Magraith Motorsport its first win of the season and lifts Matt to eighth overall. Louise, meanwhile, records her sixth victory and ninth podium of the year. Five rounds ago, after Italy, she was 183 points behind Max Cutter. Today she is three points ahead. That is a 186-point championship swing in just five races.

Andries takes his first bronze after second places in Belgium and Hungary, climbing two championship positions to twenty-first. Max scores 174 in fourth, but loses the lead for the first time since taking it in the Netherlands. JR10044 shares eighth in the race with defending champion Phil Motteram and stays third overall, 87 points behind Louise.

Adolfo Fernández Benito finishes fifth and climbs two places to eleventh, only two points behind Scott Harvey in tenth. Peter Murenu takes sixth and produces the biggest rise of the round, jumping three places from seventeenth to fourteenth. Just 28 points now cover Peter in fourteenth through Jon Evans in seventeenth, with Richard Saing and Tanuja West separated by a single point in the middle.

Phil Motteram's eighth place extends the defending champion's run to six consecutive top-eight finishes. Fooch comes home tenth and stays fifth overall, only nine points behind Jessica Dargan in fourth and 20 ahead of Peter Raimondo in sixth.

The biggest falls are shared by Simon Rogerson, Jon Evans and Kevin de Silva, all down two championship positions. FantasyGP also applied cumulative corrections of minus 10 points to Matt Magraith and minus 20 to Richard Saing; those adjustments are included in the official standings.

Eighteen rounds complete. Louise leads Max by three, JR remains within 87, and the fight from fourth to sixth is separated by only 29 points. The SAVVY championship has a new leader, and Austin has turned the final stretch into a genuine title fight.
