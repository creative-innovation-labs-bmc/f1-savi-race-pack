# 🏁 SAVI F1 League | Round 18 USA

Kimi Antonelli converted pole into a dominant United States Grand Prix victory, finishing 13.078 seconds ahead of Charles Leclerc on a one-stop strategy. Max Verstappen completed the podium, with Lewis Hamilton fourth and Lando Norris fifth. A late Virtual Safety Car followed Valtteri Bottas stopping on track, but Antonelli remained in control for his ninth win of the season.
