# 🏁 SAVI F1 League | Round 19 Mexico

Max Verstappen came from fifth to win an eventful Mexico City Grand Prix, holding off Lewis Hamilton after a red flag for Valtteri Bottas and a late Safety Car. Kimi Antonelli finished third and became Formula 1's youngest-ever World Champion. Charles Leclerc was fourth after an early five-second penalty, with Lando Norris fifth.
