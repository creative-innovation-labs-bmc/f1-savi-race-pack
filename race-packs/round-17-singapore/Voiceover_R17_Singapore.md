# SAVVY F1 League | Round 17 Singapore voice-over

Round 17 took Formula 1 to Singapore, where Max Verstappen finally conquered Marina Bay. On his 250th Grand Prix start, Verstappen defended from Charles Leclerc into Turn One and controlled the race despite radio problems forcing Red Bull to use the pit board. Two Virtual Safety Cars interrupted the strategy, but Max stayed clear for his first Singapore victory and the 73rd win of his career. Leclerc finished second, while Kimi Antonelli passed Lewis Hamilton late to take third.

Now to the SAVVY League, where Jessica Dargan has delivered the highest score of the round at exactly the right time. AloeVira wins Singapore with 263 points, Jessica's second victory and fourth podium of the season. She climbs two places to fourth overall, 14 points ahead of Fooch. After Madrid she was 110 behind him. Three races later, that battle has swung by 124 points.

Louise Mihalev finishes second on 252, ending a run of three straight wins but extending an even more important streak: four consecutive podiums. Louise has now stood on the podium in seven of the last eight rounds. She takes 40 points out of Max Cutter in Singapore and cuts his championship lead from 52 points to just 12. After Italy, Louise was 183 behind. Four races later, that deficit is down to 12, a 171-point swing. She also moves clear of JR10044 in second and now leads JR by 55.

Michael Sideris takes third with 227 for his first podium of the season. He climbs two championship places to twelfth, only three points behind the tie for tenth. Phil Motteram and Peter Murenu share fourth on 223. For defending champion Phil, that makes five consecutive top-five finishes: fifth in Italy, second in Madrid, third in Baku, fifth in Bahrain and now fourth in Singapore. Peter adds another top-four result after his second place in Azerbaijan.

Scott Harvey finishes sixth and climbs one place into a tie for tenth with Simon Rogerson. Tanuja West takes seventh, championship leader Max is eighth, and Jean Alesi shares ninth with Martin West.

Fooch finishes twenty-fifth after twenty-ninth in Baku and twenty-first in Bahrain, slipping to fifth overall. Richard Saing, third in last year's championship, records the biggest fall, dropping three places to fourteenth. Charlotte Wareham, last year's runner-up, falls two places to twenty-fifth.

Seventeen rounds complete. Max still leads, but Louise is only 12 points away, Jessica is up to fourth, and just three points separate shared tenth from twelfth. Singapore has turned the title fight into a sprint.
