# 🏁 SAVI F1 League | Round 20 Brazil

Lando Norris won a wet-dry-wet São Paulo Grand Prix from pole, finishing 14.6 seconds ahead of Max Verstappen after the Red Bull driver charged from 21st on the grid. Isack Hadjar completed a Red Bull double podium, while fourth and fifth for George Russell and Kimi Antonelli secured the Constructors’ Championship for Mercedes.
