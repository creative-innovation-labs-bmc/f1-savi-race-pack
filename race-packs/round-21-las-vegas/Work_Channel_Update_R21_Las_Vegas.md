# 🏁 SAVI F1 League | Round 21 Las Vegas

Max Verstappen won an action-packed Las Vegas Grand Prix from pole, repeatedly retaking the lead during a race-long fight with Mercedes. Late problems dropped George Russell to ninth and Kimi Antonelli to fourth, allowing Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri to complete a McLaren double podium.
